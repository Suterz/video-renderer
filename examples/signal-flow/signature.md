# Pocket Cutouts
## Illustration
Original bold plum ink, rounded wedge silhouettes, ivory paper, petrol-teal shadows and gentle gold object highlights. Expressive but very simple compositions. Generous spacing for separate illustrated objects. No writing, labels, numerals, wires, signal rays, arrows, pointers, or lines that connect the objects. Preserve object identity and camera between successive panels.

## Renderer-only graphics
The renderer adds exact labels, numbers and gold directional arrows with plum keylines. Only introduce a connection at the specified narration cue.
