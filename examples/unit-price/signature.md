# Pocket Cutouts

## Illustration
Original phone-first editorial cartoon: bold, irregular plum brush contours with blunt ends and rounded wedge-shaped objects. People have expressive eyebrows, compact bodies, generous hands and distinct silhouettes. Warm ivory paper; apricot focal colors, petrol-teal shadows, one simple folded-paper shadow under each form, faint dry grain in empty space. Sparse background detail and a stable camera within a scene. No existing cartoon franchise or imitation of a named artist. Leave an uncluttered lower band. Do not draw any symbols, decorative lettering, numbers, signage, visual effects, price tags with writing, or pointers. Maintain the same ink weight, color rhythm and simple geometry even when the characters and setting change.

## Renderer-only graphics
Important labels, prices, counts, diagram lines and arrows are added by the renderer after artwork is accepted. Use Noto Sans Bold, cream type on plum blocks, and chunky gold arrows with plum outlines. Never bake essential lettering into artwork. Tell the story through held images and one visual change at a time.
