# V3 evaluation contract

One valid public POST per case and run. Archive body, response, job ID, exact
runtime SHAs, harness/input revision, settings, and first outcome. Never count a
caller correction or retry as success. Internal declared panel repairs are allowed.
Original H jobs are never retried, selected, or overwritten. Use fresh service
stores, no fixture-specific cached artifacts. Compare on the semantic brief where
the public payload must differ between v2 and v3.

Required: all expected setup marks visible at frame zero; question occurs before
the mechanism; at least 1.25 s between the aligned last question word and the
mechanism (minor quantization tolerance 80 ms); no answer-only mark before the
conclusion unless independently demonstrated as part of the mechanism. The final
encoded frame holds the whole takeaway. Keep the request's final hold; all videos
are strictly under 45 s. Decode actual before/after frames and audio. Validate
fonts/readability on 360x640 and actual legibility/associations, not just overlay
strings. A naked assertion styled as an illustration does not satisfy the fixture.
For generated scenes check named parts and continuity with neutral observations or
review the decoded images; human audience comprehension and listening stay separate.

For deterministic teaching graphics measure intended marks against the raster:
counter instances from non-overlapping regions, ruler tick count and equal spacing,
bar dimensions relative to the stated baseline, connector start/tip and route,
and real axes/endpoints. Compare authored arithmetic, counts, and transforms
independently of encoded media and flag a wrong fixture as invalid evidence rather
than silently adjusting a check. OCR is independent corroboration, not proof of
meaning. Manual visual judgments may adjudicate ambiguous OCR without changing
the request. If neither is available, the outcome stays unjudged.

Accepted output plus a demonstrated required-check failure is false acceptance.
`needs_review` on valid input is a safe refusal, never a pass. Infrastructure
errors, unjudged, and unrun are separate. First-candidate pass needs a verified
one-shot pass and complete attempt evidence; missing telemetry is unknown.

For H01 check association of rate and volume plus the 12 / 9 comparison, H02
four connected layers (not sheets), H03 right pan and fixed pivot, H04 return
direction and complete order, H05 slider rather than pull tab, H06 diameter and
distance ratios and one turn/contact evidence, H07 horizontal lengths 6/3/1,
H08 unique permutation B/C/A, H09 equal offsets about the mirror in both states,
and H10 three labels but exactly two counted intervals. These evidence checks
are required even if the auto-rendering declares acceptance.
